Core Identity

Height & Weight: Physical dimensions (affects moves like Grass Knot or Heavy Slam).

Egg Group: Biological classification determining breeding compatibility.

Combat Stats
Base Stats: The species' natural strengths in HP, Attack, Defense, Sp. Atk, Sp. Def, and Speed.

IVs (Individual Values): Hidden genetic numbers (0-31) affecting stat potential.

EVs (Effort Values): Training points gained from battles that boost specific stats.

Nature: Personality trait that typically boosts one stat by 10% and hinders another.

Abilities & Moves
Ability: A passive trait that aids in battle or exploration (e.g., Levitate for ground immunity).

Moveset: The four active techniques the Pokémon knows.

Field Moves (HMs): Historic capability to interact with the environment (e.g., Fly, Surf, Cut).

Movement & Terrain
Movement Capabilities: Can it fly, swim, or dig? (Often defined by Type or Ability).
